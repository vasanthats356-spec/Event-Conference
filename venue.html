Venue FAQs

Is there a cloakroom facility?

Yes, complimentary cloakroom services are available near the main entrance and in the Expo Hall. Please collect your items before 7:00 PM daily.

Can I bring a bag into the sessions?

Small bags and backpacks are permitted in the session halls. However, large luggage must be checked at the cloakroom.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Absolutely. The venue features ramps, elevators, and accessible restrooms in all halls. If you require special assistance, please reach out to our accessibility desk upon arrival.

Are there accessible parking spots?

Yes, dedicated accessible parking is available near the main entrance. Please display your valid permit.

Can I bring my own food to the venue?

Outside food and beverages are generally not permitted, except for medical or dietary reasons. Please contact our support team in advance if you have specific requirements.

Are there vegan options available?

Yes, our Grand Food Court offers a wide variety of clearly labeled vegan and gluten-free options.
